# google-cybersecurity-portfolio

## About me
My curiosity for how systems work, combined with a strong sense of ethics around protecting people's data, drives my transition into cybersecurity. As a working professional upskilling through the Google Cybersecurity Certificate, I bring an analytical mindset and a commitment to continuous learning to every challenge I encounter. I aim to channel these strengths, along with my developer experience, into entry-level security work — monitoring logs, following playbooks, and escalating issues — to help mitigate threats and prevent data breaches before they happen.
